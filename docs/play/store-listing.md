### English (~1800 caratteri)

```
Kite or no kite? The app answers that question and nothing else.

No charts to decode at 7am: open it, read the spot verdict, and know whether it's worth loading the gear in the car.

THE VERDICT, NOT JUST DATA
• Instant go/no-go for every spot, computed from wind, gusts and direction
• Two independent weather models cross-checked: DWD ICON (German weather service) and NOAA GFS (US) — when the models disagree, it tells you instead of faking certainty
• Gust analysis: a gusty 18 knots is not a clean 18 knots, and the verdict treats it accordingly

DIRECTION SAFETY
• Every spot knows its sea direction: the app tells you if the wind is onshore, side or offshore, and how safe that is
• Offshore = a clear warning, no sugar-coating

THE RIGHT SIZE
• Kite size recommendation based on your weight and the forecast wind
• Enter the kites you own: it picks the right one from your quiver, not a theoretical size

YOUR SPOTS
• Built-in spots for Margherita di Savoia and Zapponeta (Puglia, Italy)
• Add any spot with coordinates and sea direction: the verdict works anywhere
• Hour-by-hour forecast for 2 days, wave height and period included

MAP AND LIVE STATIONS
• Animated wind map (Windy) built in
• Direct links to wind24.it live wind stations where available

BUILT THE RIGHT WAY
• No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking
• No location permission: you choose the spots
• Your data (spots, weight, quiver) stays on your device
• Works offline with the last downloaded data
• Free

Made by a kiter tired of opening four apps to make one decision. The wind won't wait: kite or no kite.
```
